# Pass 1: Gate Cancellation & Rotation Merging (`CancellationPass`)

## 1. Overview & Mathematical Foundations

`CancellationPass` operates natively on Qiskit's `DAGCircuit` representation. It traverses the circuit DAG to eliminate redundant quantum operations via two core reduction mechanisms:
1. **Inverse Gate Pair Cancellation**
2. **Rotation Merging & Identity Elimination**

---

### 1.1 Rule 1a: Inverse Gate Pair Cancellation

Two unitary gates $U_A$ and $U_B$ acting on identical qubit registers satisfy cancellation if $U_B = U_A^\dagger$, since:
$$U_B \cdot U_A = U_A^\dagger \cdot U_A = I$$

#### Supported Involutory (Self-Inverse) Gates
For any Hermitian unitary $U = U^\dagger = U^{-1}$, applying the gate twice reduces to the identity:
$$U^2 = I$$
- **Single-qubit**:
  - Hadamard: $H^2 = I$
  - Pauli-$X$: $X^2 = I$
  - Pauli-$Y$: $Y^2 = I$
  - Pauli-$Z$: $Z^2 = I$
- **Two-qubit**:
  - Controlled-NOT ($CX$ / $CNOT$): $CX^2 = I$ (matching control and target wires)
  - Controlled-$Z$ ($CZ$): $CZ^2 = I$
  - Swap ($SWAP$): $SWAP^2 = I$

#### Supported Conjugate Transpose Inverse Pairs
- Phase gate $S$ and $S^\dagger$:
  $$S = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & i \end{pmatrix}, \quad S^\dagger = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -i \end{pmatrix} \implies S S^\dagger = S^\dagger S = I$$
- $T$ gate and $T^\dagger$:
  $$T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & e^{i\pi/4} \end{pmatrix}, \quad T^\dagger = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & e^{-i\pi/4} \end{pmatrix} \implies T T^\dagger = T^\dagger T = I$$
- Square-root of $X$ ($SX$) and $SX^\dagger$:
  $$SX = \frac{1}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 1+i & 1-i \\ 1-i & 1+i \end{pmatrix}, \quad SX^\dagger = (SX)^\dagger \implies SX \cdot SX^\dagger = SX^\dagger \cdot SX = I$$

#### Basis Compaction Rule
- Successive $SX$ gates on the same wire combine directly into Pauli-$X$:
  $$SX \cdot SX = \sqrt{X} \cdot \sqrt{X} = X$$
  This replaces two 1-qubit basis operations with a single Pauli-$X$ gate.

---

### 1.2 Rule 1b: Continuous Rotation Merging & Identity Elimination

For any 1-qubit rotation generated by Pauli matrix $\sigma \in \{X, Y, Z\}$:
$$R_\sigma(\theta) = \exp\left(-i \frac{\theta}{2} \sigma\right)$$

Because rotations along the same axis commute, the product of adjacent rotations on the same qubit is additive in angle:
$$R_\sigma(\theta_1) R_\sigma(\theta_2) = \exp\left(-i \frac{\theta_1}{2} \sigma\right) \exp\left(-i \frac{\theta_2}{2} \sigma\right) = \exp\left(-i \frac{\theta_1 + \theta_2}{2} \sigma\right) = R_\sigma(\theta_1 + \theta_2)$$

Similarly, for the Phase gate $P(\lambda) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & e^{i\lambda} \end{pmatrix}$:
$$P(\lambda_1) P(\lambda_2) = P(\lambda_1 + \lambda_2)$$

#### Identity Elimination (Zero-Angle Removal)
Whenever the accumulated angle satisfies:
$$\theta_{\text{total}} \equiv 0 \pmod{2\pi}$$
the rotation simplifies to global identity $R_\sigma(\theta) = \pm I$.
The pass computes:
$$\theta_{\text{rem}} = |\theta_{\text{total}}| \pmod{2\pi}$$
If $\theta_{\text{rem}} < 10^{-9}$ or $|2\pi - \theta_{\text{rem}}| < 10^{-9}$, the rotation node is completely removed from the DAG without replacement.

## 3. Strict Safety Invariants & Disallowed Cases

`CancellationPass` enforces conservative graph-theoretic and semantic checks. A rewrite **must NOT** be applied in any of the following scenarios:

1. **Non-Adjacent Gates (Intervening Operations)**:
   - For single-qubit gates, the successor on the wire must be the exact candidate node. If any gate, barrier, or measurement intervenes, no cancellation can occur.
   - For multi-qubit gates ($CX, CZ, SWAP$), strict wire-by-wire adjacency is required: for *all* wires $q \in \text{qargs}(A)$, the immediate quantum successor of $A$ on wire $q$ must be node $B$, and the immediate quantum predecessor of $B$ on wire $q$ must be node $A$.

2. **Wire Role Mismatch**:
   - For asymmetric two-qubit gates like $CX$, the control and target indices must match exactly:
     $$CX(q_0, q_1) \cdot CX(q_1, q_0) \ne I$$
     Cancellation between $CX(0, 1)$ and $CX(1, 0)$ is strictly forbidden.

3. **Symbolic Parameters**:
   - If either rotation node contains an unresolved symbolic `Parameter` or `ParameterExpression` (`node.is_parameterized() == True`), the pass skips merging to preserve symbolic expression trees.

4. **Different Rotation Axes**:
   - $RX(\theta_1)$ and $RZ(\theta_2)$ do not commute:
     $$[R_X, R_Z] \ne 0$$
     Merging is strictly restricted to operations sharing the same rotation generator.

5. **Directives & Classical Instructions**:
   - Barriers (`barrier`), measurements (`measure`), resets (`reset`), conditioned operations (`condition is not None`), or classical registers (`len(node.cargs) > 0`) act as hard boundaries and are never cancelled or merged across.

6. **Fixed-Point Convergence**:
   - The pass runs iteratively until an entire traversal of the DAG produces zero modifications, guaranteeing that cascading cancellations (e.g., $H - X - X - H \to H - H \to \emptyset$) are fully resolved.
